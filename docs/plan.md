# OSRS music map player

Personal, local-only page. Click an area on the world map, hear the music track
that unlocks there. Pure HTML/CSS/JS, no build, no server. All assets hotlinked
or cropped from reference screenshots.

## Layout

Map pane left (2/3), sidebar right (1/3).

- Map: official OSRS world map JPG as base image. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom.
  Polygons from the wiki music map drawn in an SVG that shares the map's
  transform. Hover tints the area and shows its name. Click plays its track.
  Area of the playing track stays highlighted.
- Sidebar: stone-ring frame. Now playing on parchment (name, unlock text,
  length, release, members or free). Controls, search, era and type filters,
  full track list. Clicking a list item also pans the map to that track's area
  when it has one.

## Data

- `tracks.js`: 853 tracks scraped from User:Cook_Me_Plox/music.
- `areas.js`: generated by `scripts/build-areas.py` from the wiki
  Map:Music_tracks mapdata API. Each area: name, track indices, polygon in game
  coordinates. Multi-track regions keep all their tracks; click plays the first
  and the sidebar lists the rest.
- Game coordinate to image pixel mapping: linear, two constants per axis,
  calibrated once against landmarks, stored as CSS/JS constants.

## Filters

"Up to 2007" is default. Filtered-out tracks are hidden in the list and their
areas are hidden on the map.

## Style

Stone brick wall background (CSS). Sidebar frame, parchment and button textures
cropped from the reference screenshots into `assets/`. RuneScape font hotlinked
from the wiki. Colours and sizes as custom properties on `:root`.

## Files

- `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js` (player, list, filters), `map.js`
  (pan, zoom, polygons, hit test), `tracks.js`, `areas.js`, `assets/`,
  `scripts/build-areas.py`.

## Verify

Open in Chrome. Click Lumbridge: Harmony plays and the list highlights it.
Search "sea", next, shuffle, filters still work.

## Playback context (added later)

Spotify-style context plus queue. Clicking a map area or a track inside a zone
group sets the context to that zone: its tracks, alphabetical, filters applied.
Next and auto-advance walk the zone, then continue alphabetically through the
next zone in level order (wrapping around). Clicking a track in the flat list means
context "All music". Shuffle stays inside the zone until every track played.
The now-playing box shows "From", the zone queue (hidden above 15 tracks) and
"Next".

## Zones

`ZONES` in `areas.js`: every track in exactly one zone. Named wiki regions
first, then the region polygon that contains a track's own polygon, then coarse
kingdom boxes, then place names in the unlock text. Rest is "Elsewhere".
Dungeon and instance polygons sit outside the official image, so they are
hidden on the map and focusing such a track zooms all the way out.

## Levels

No level gates exist in OSRS, so `LEVELS` in `build-areas.py` is a hand table
of typical mob combat levels per zone. Grouped list sorts zones by the midpoint
of that range, unknown zones after, Elsewhere last. Shown in zone headers and
map tooltips.

## Later additions

x 2048, y 4160), zoom and locate buttons, faint zone highlight on hover,
